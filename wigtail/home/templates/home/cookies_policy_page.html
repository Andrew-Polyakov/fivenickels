This policy describes how this site uses cookies and similar technologies, and the choices available to you. It is separate from our general privacy practices; contact details for privacy requests appear in your site’s privacy notice if one is published.

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels used for measurement or security. Cookies can be session (removed when you close the browser) or persistent (they remain until they expire or you delete them).

How we use cookies

We group cookies and similar technologies into the following categories:

  1. Necessary. Required for core site operation, security, load balancing, fraud prevention, and (where applicable) authenticated areas such as the Wagtail admin. These cannot be switched off through our site alone.
  2. Performance and analytics. Used in aggregate to understand how pages are used and to improve stability and performance. We aim to keep this category minimal.
  3. Functional. Remember choices such as language or layout preferences when we explicitly offer those features.
  4. Third-party content and embeds. Some pages load scripts or widgets from other providers; those providers may set their own cookies or identifiers under their policies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the site (including sign-in) from working. For third-party cookies, you can also use vendor-specific opt-out tools where available, in addition to browser controls.

Changes

We may update this policy when we add features, change vendors, or when laws require clearer disclosure. The updated version will be posted on this page; review it periodically if cookie use matters to your compliance or preferences.
